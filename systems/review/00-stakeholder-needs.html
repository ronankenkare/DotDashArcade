Stakeholder Needs

Dot Dash stakeholder needs

What players need from Dot Dash, in their own terms. These are the top of the trace tree: each system requirement in 01 Requirements refines one or more of these needs, and the manual acceptance checks in 04 Verification Plan validate them.

NEED-001 Quick offline play

A player can open the app on iPhone or iPad and be playing a short reflex game within seconds, with no account, purchase or network connection.

NEED-002 Fair, escalating challenge

Success depends only on the player's timing, the rules are easy to read, and the game gets harder as the player succeeds, with a gentler and a harder variant to choose from.

NEED-003 Durable progress

A player's best result for each variant survives closing the app and updates, and is never lowered or lost.

NEED-004 Interruption-safe play

A call, notification or switch to another app never lets the game keep running unattended or produce a result the player did not earn.

NEED-005 Comfortable presentation and feedback

The player can choose a light or dark presentation and gets clear visual and haptic feedback on success and failure.

NEED-006 Help when needed

The player can reach the developer for help from inside the app.
